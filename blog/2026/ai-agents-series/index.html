This series documents my learning journey with AI agents, informed primarily by hands-on experience using Codex across a range of projects.

The field is advancing—and the ways we work with agents are changing—so quickly that I often feel as though I am struggling to keep up. For me, writing this blog is a way to capture the experiences and observations I gather along the way. It helps me synthesize and share ideas I find useful, while also providing a constructive way to acknowledge and manage the anxiety that comes with such rapid change.
